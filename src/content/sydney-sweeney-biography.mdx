Sydney Bernice Sweeney was born on **September 12, 1997**, in Spokane, Washington, United States. She spent much of her childhood in the Pacific Northwest, where she developed an early interest in storytelling, sports, and performing.

Unlike many child actors who grew up in entertainment families, Sweeney came from a relatively traditional background. She has often spoken about how supportive her family was when she expressed an interest in acting.

To convince her parents to let her pursue an acting career, she reportedly prepared a detailed business plan explaining how acting could become a realistic profession. That determination became one of the defining characteristics of her career.

Soon afterward, her family relocated to Los Angeles so she could pursue professional acting opportunities.
